Roadmap to the platinum

Difficulty 4/10 · about 200 hours · one playthrough · 0 online trophies · 1 potentially missable. Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off. There is no mission replay, so save manually at the start and end of every chapter.

Stage 1 — Play the story 23 trophies · 60–80 h

Finish the 5 chapters and the story missions. Do the Chapter 1 meditation for Positive Mental Attitude (the only missable trophy) before Chapter 1 ends. Try activities as they appear, scan and drive new vehicles, and start the stranger and character strands as they unlock.

Stage 2 — 100% completion 23 trophies · ~100 h

After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion for the in-game checklist. Clear the robberies, collectibles (cards, WAiNK, underwater, flamingos), races, stunts and challenges. Nothing on the checklist is missable.

Stage 3 — Clean-up 8 trophies · ~10 h

Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands, the character final missions, and whatever is left on your list. Then Vice Got a Price! pops with the other 54.

⚠ Missable: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate with Lucia on the balcony at Jason's house in Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth. It is the only trophy any guide flags as potentially missable.
